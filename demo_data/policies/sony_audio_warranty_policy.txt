                    SONY CONSUMER AUDIO LIMITED WARRANTY POLICY
                          Effective Date: January 1, 2026
================================================================================

1. SCOPE OF COVERAGE
Sony Electronics Inc. ("Sony") warrants this audio product (including WH-1000 series
headphones) against defects in material or workmanship for a period of one (1) year 
from the original date of purchase.

2. ELIGIBLE DEFECTS
• Spontaneous shutdown, power failure, or battery charging irregularities not caused by user damage.
• Acoustic distortion, driver failure, or Active Noise Cancellation (ANC) sensor malfunctions.
• Firmware communication errors unresponsive to factory reset procedures.

3. CLAIM REQUIREMENTS
To initiate a valid warranty replacement or repair claim, the claimant must provide:
(a) Proof of original purchase (receipt or invoice showing merchant name, date, and purchase price).
(b) Product model and valid serial number.
(c) Detailed description of the symptoms and troubleshooting steps attempted.

4. REMEDY & TURNAROUND
Upon receipt of claim documentation, Sony will issue a prepaid shipping label. Sony will,
at its option, replace the defective unit with a certified new or refurbished unit of
equal value ($179.00 MSRP), or issue a full refund within 5–7 business days.

5. CONTACT FOR CLAIMS
Direct submissions: warranty-claims@sony-electronics-support.example.com
Reference Claim Portal: https://claims.sony-audio.example.com/portal
